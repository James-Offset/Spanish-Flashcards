How to use

  1. Read the Spanish and try to remember the meaning.
  2. Tap the card to see the English (and the clue, if there is one). If a card has a clue, you can tap 💡 Show clue first for a hint.
  3. Pick a level if you want to change how often the card comes up:
    • Frequent: about 3 times per round (new words start here)
    • Infrequent: once per round
    • Learned: about 1 round in 4
    A level change applies from the next round.
  4. ✎ Add clue lets you write your own hint for a card.
  5. Tap Next card to move on. Your place is saved, so you can close the app any time and carry on later.

Levels and clues you set here are saved on this phone. To copy them into your word list on your PC, use Menu → Send progress to my PC now and then. The header shows how many changes haven't been sent yet.

Send progress to your PC

  1. Tap Share file below and send it to yourself (Google Drive, email, etc.).
  2. On your PC, save it as progress.json in the Spanish Flashcards folder.
  3. Open a terminal in that folder and run:
    python flashcards.py import progress.json
  4. Push to GitHub, as the command tells you. The app picks up the changes next time it opens.
