How to use

Two ways to practise

  • Spanish → English: you see the Spanish and recall the meaning.
  • English → Spanish: you see the English and try to say the Spanish.
Switch with the buttons at the top. Each mode keeps its own place, levels and clues.

Each card

  1. Tap the card to see the answer (and the clue, if there is one). If a card has a clue, you can tap 💡 Show clue first for a hint.
  2. Pick a level if you want to change how often the card comes up in this mode:
    • Frequent: about 3 times per round (new words start here)
    • Infrequent: once per round
    • Learned: about 1 round in 4
    A level change applies from the next round.
  3. ✎ Add clue lets you write your own hint for this card in this mode.
  4. Tap Next card to move on. Your place is saved, so you can close the app any time and carry on later.

Keeping your PC up to date

Levels and clues you set here are saved on this phone. Now and then, use Menu → Send progress to my PC to copy them into your word list. The top bar shows how many changes haven't been sent yet.

Send progress to your PC

  1. Tap Share file below and send it to yourself (Google Drive, email, etc.).
  2. On your PC, save the file. Your Downloads folder or the Spanish Flashcards folder both work.
  3. In the Spanish Flashcards folder, double-click Update flashcards.bat.
  4. In GitHub Desktop, click Commit to main, then Push origin. The app picks up the changes a few minutes later.

Your clue

A hint that helps you remember. It only shows in this mode. Leave it empty to remove the clue.
